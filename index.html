What this PoC is doing

The camera freezes one scene containing the horizon and cloud. Instead of covering targets with your finger, drag that frozen image beneath the fixed crosshair and mark horizon, base, top, left, and right. Because every mark belongs to one image, camera geometry remains valid and CloudWatch can render the exact measurement back as an annotated receipt.
