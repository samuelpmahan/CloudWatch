What this PoC is doing

Tap the visible horizon, then the cloud base, top, left edge, and right edge. The horizon gives a real 0° elevation reference in the camera frame, avoiding flaky browser phone-pitch sensors. Surface temperature and dew point estimate lifting-condensation-level cloud base; camera geometry then estimates range and size.
